<div class="about-grid">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>I am a <a href="https://www.grappa.amsterdam/">GRAPPA</a> Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Amsterdam, working within the groups of <a href="https://gianfrancobertone.net/">Gianfranco Bertone</a> and <a href="https://samayanissanke.wordpress.com/">Samaya Nissanke</a>.</p>
    <p>I use perturbative methods in general relativity to model how matter and fundamental fields interact with black holes and compact binaries. I test these models against numerical relativity simulations and use Bayesian inference to compare their predictions with observations.</p>
    <p>Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in <a href="https://www.icrea.cat/community/icreas/29841/diego-blas-temino/">Diego Blas</a>’s group at the <a href="https://www.ifae.es/">Institute for High Energy Physics (IFAE)</a> in Barcelona, where I held a Juan de la Cierva Fellowship. I completed my PhD at <a href="https://centra.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/network/grit/">CENTRA, Instituto Superior Técnico</a> in Lisbon, supervised by <a href="https://vitorcardoso.strong-gr.com/">Vitor Cardoso</a>.</p>
